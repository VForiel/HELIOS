# Redesign of Adaptive Optics and Atmosphere Icons

## Summary
Reworked the SVG icons for `AdaptiveOptics` and `Atmosphere` to be more visually descriptive and consistent with the project's style.

## Changes
- **`src/helios/assets/adaptive_optics.svg`**:
  - Replaced the abstract representation with a schematic of a Deformable Mirror (DM).
  - Shows actuators, a deformable surface, and incoming/outgoing wavefronts.
  - Uses the standard color palette (Blue/Grey/Green/Red).

- **`src/helios/assets/atmosphere.svg`**:
  - Updated to a 100x100 viewBox.
  - Represents turbulence with multiple wavy layers of varying opacity.
  - Added "eddies" and a distorted light ray to symbolize the atmospheric effect.

## Verification
- The SVG files are valid XML.
- They follow the 100x100 viewBox convention.
- Colors match the existing palette.
