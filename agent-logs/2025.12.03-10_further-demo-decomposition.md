# Further Decomposition of Demo Scripts

## Summary
Further decomposed the example scripts to separate distinct functionalities, specifically separating SED visualization from scene geometry, and pupil construction from diffraction pattern calculation.

## Changes
- **Split `01_scene_observation.py`** into:
  - `01_scene_geometry.py`: Focuses on scene setup and spatial visualization.
  - `02_spectral_energy_distribution.py`: Focuses on plotting SEDs of objects.
- **Split `02_telescope_pupil.py`** into:
  - `03_pupil_construction.py`: Focuses on manual and preset pupil creation.
  - `04_diffraction_pattern.py`: Focuses on PSF calculation and visualization.
- **Renamed `03_interferometry.py`** to `05_interferometry_arrays.py`.
- **Split `04_atmospheric_turbulence.py`** into:
  - `06_atmospheric_phase_screen.py`: Static phase screen generation.
  - `07_frozen_flow_turbulence.py`: Temporal evolution simulation.
- **Renumbered remaining scripts**:
  - `08_atmospheric_animation.py` (was 05)
  - `09_coronagraphy.py` (was 06)
  - `10_chromatic_psf.py` (was 07)
  - `11_end_to_end_simulation.py` (was 08)
  - `12_uml_visualization.py` (was 09)
  - `13_photonic_circuit.py` (was 10)
  - `14_photonics_uml_generation.py` (was 11)

## Verification
- Old combined scripts were removed.
- New scripts are numbered sequentially from 01 to 14.
- Each script is self-contained.
