# Off-Axis Phase Tilt Fix

## Summary of Changes
- Modified `TelescopeArray.process` in `src/helios/components/collector.py`:
    - Implemented the geometric phase tilt calculation for off-axis sources.
    - Previously, only the piston term (phase shift at collector center) was applied.
    - Now, a tilt term $\phi_{tilt} = k (u \theta_x + v \theta_y)$ is added, where $(u, v)$ are local coordinates across the collector aperture.
    - This ensures that off-axis wavefronts are correctly modeled as tilted plane waves incident on each collector, not just phase-shifted flat waves.

## Files Modified
- `src/helios/components/collector.py`

## Tests
- Created and ran `tests/repro_tilt.py` (deleted after verification):
    - Confirmed that phase was initially flat (0 rad diff) for off-axis sources.
    - Verified that after the fix, the phase difference across the pupil matches the expected geometric tilt ($k \Delta x \theta$).
    - Used a small angle (0.01 arcsec) to avoid phase wrapping ambiguity during verification.
